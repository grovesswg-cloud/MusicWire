ABOUT

The music industry is a beat.
We cover it.

MusicWire is an independent online music publication built for journalists, by journalists. We believe the music industry is one of the most complex, fascinating, and underreported beats in the world — and we intend to cover it properly.

That means speed without sloppiness. Opinion without tribalism. Data without dehumanisation. We break news, publish criticism that earns its position, and document the business of music as rigorously as its art.

MusicWire is not a fan site. It is not a PR extension. It is a publication. We have editors. We have standards. We get things wrong and we correct them on the record. That is what journalism looks like.

Core Commitments

SPEED

We publish when the story is ready, not when it is perfect — and we correct on the record.

ACCURACY

Every fact is sourced. Every claim is attributed. Corrections are published at the top, not the bottom.

INDEPENDENCE

We take no direction from labels, managers, or advertisers. Our reviews are not for sale.

ACCESS

MusicWire is free to read. The music industry is everyone's story. We don't paywall the news.

The Wire Scale

MusicWire reviews are rated on the Wire Scale — five values, each a transmission strength:

STATIC
Competent. Does what it says. Average.
SIGNAL
Strong. A clear transmission worth hearing.
BREAKING
Essential. Stop what you're doing.
TRANSMISSION
Rare. Career-defining. Alters the conversation.
FREQUENCY
Once in a generation. Assigned sparingly.

Editorial Standards

MusicWire clearly delineates news reporting from critical opinion from analysis. All opinion is labeled. All sources are named. MusicWire does not publish anonymous sources, and does not publish corrections at the bottom of pieces where they will not be seen.
